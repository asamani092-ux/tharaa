Part 1: Technical Issues & Required Modifications
1. Existing Bugs to Patch:

Deletion Failure: The backend fails to execute delete operations; records are not being removed from the database.

Quota (Nisab) Sync Failure: The system fails to dynamically update and synchronize the "Quota" value when associated parameters change.

Data Injection Defect: The frontend fails to fetch and inject the "Quota" value into the designated input fields within the "Weekly Progress" tracking form UI.

2. Supervisor Dashboard UI/UX Refactoring:

Overview Tab: Completely remove the "Pending Requests" UI card.

Participants Management Tab:

Implement an "Edit Data" feature for individual participants.

Debug and resolve the programmatic error in the "Delete" button.

Curriculum Tab: Implement an "Edit Book Data" feature directly linked to the database.

Batches (Cohorts) Tab:

Implement an "Edit Batch Details" feature.

Implement a "Delete Entire Batch" feature, ensuring a cascade delete to remove all student records associated with that batch.

Settings Tab: Add a dedicated "Supervisor Profile" section allowing updates to the Admin Code, Admin Name, and the Login Mobile Number.

3. Submission Time Logic Refactoring:

Utilize the "Day" as a complete time unit (24 hours).

Deprecate/Remove the "End of Submission Time" variable.

Define a "Start Day of Submission" (e.g., Friday). This day grants a full 24-hour window for a "Standard Submission."

Any submission on the subsequent day (e.g., Saturday) is automatically flagged as a "Late Submission."

Add a configuration to "Select All Days" as standard submission days. Enabling this bypasses the "Late Submission" logic entirely (acting as a fail-safe to prevent API messaging issues).

Part 2: Supervisor Scenario (System Architect)
1. Login and Initial Setup (Program Launch Day):

The supervisor opens sam-dev.win and is greeted by the elegant "Tharaa Al-Maarifa" UI in navy and gold.

They log in using their mobile number and password, seamlessly routing to the "Supervisor Dashboard" without page reloads (SPA architecture).

They navigate to the "Settings" tab, set the "Weekly Quota" to 75 pages, and define the submission time logic (e.g., Start: Friday, End: Wednesday for standard, Thursday flagged as late).

2. Crowd Management (Participant Acceptance):

The supervisor switches to the "Join Requests" tab. They select 5 self-registered students, click "Bulk Accept," and assign them to Batch (1-2026).

They have an external Excel sheet with 50 students. They click "Bulk Add," paste the copied list (Name, Mobile, Password) into the text area, and assign them to Batch (2-2026). With one click, the system parses the payload, creates accounts, and allocates them instantly.

3. Weekly Monitoring & Analytics Harvesting:

Three weeks later, the supervisor accesses the "Analytics" tab.

A live dashboard is displayed. They filter by "Batch 1-2026."

They instantly see that student "Ahmed" has 3 standard submissions with a 100% rating. Student "Saad" has 1 late submission, 1 missing submission, and a 60% rating.

Clicking "Export Excel" generates a clean, formatted report ready for upper management to validate the program's success.

Part 3: Participant Scenario (The Seamless Reader Journey)
1. Onboarding & Curriculum Familiarization:

The student opens the site on their mobile device and logs in. The system persists their session (no re-login required for the upcoming weeks).

The "Participant Portal" opens. The system intelligently filters distractions, displaying only the books assigned to their specific phase ("Phase 1 - Basic Level").

The student clicks on the book "Osool Al-Eman" (e.g., 200 pages). A "Download PDF" button is available, followed by an "Approve/Start" button to initialize their progress tracking for this book.

2. The First Weekly Submission (Standard Tracking):

Wednesday arrives. The student logs in and sees an elegant UI card: "Currently Reading: Osool Al-Eman. You stopped at page 0."

The system auto-calculates and injects the target into the input fields: (From page 1 .. To page 75).

The student actually read up to page 75, so they leave the numbers as is.

They click "Add Benefit/Reflection" and type: "Today I learned that faith is words and actions."

They click "Share via WhatsApp," triggering a deep link that opens WhatsApp to instantly share the reflection with peers.

Returning to the app, they click "Submit Progress." A quick green Toast notification appears: "Your progress has been successfully recorded, bless your efforts!"

3. The Exceptional Week (The Magic of Completion & Quota Rollover):

In the third week, the student previously reached page 150.

The system suggests the target this week is to finish the book at page 200, plus an additional 25 pages to fulfill their quota! (Since the quota is 75, and only 50 pages remained in the current book).

The student finishes the book and reads 25 pages from a new one.

They input page 200 and click "Submit Progress."

Here, the system's engineering brilliance kicks in: Instead of a standard success toast, the screen dims and a golden Modal pops up:

"Congratulations! You have completed the book 'Osool Al-Eman'! 🎉 \n But wait... you still have 25 pages left in your weekly quota. Select your next book from the dropdown below to roll over your progress."

The student selects "Tafseer Juz Amma" from the dropdown and clicks "Confirm."

In a split second: The "Osool Al-Eman" book card turns green with a (Completed) badge, the student is instantly enrolled in the new book, and the 25-page progress is successfully logged into the new book's state without any further manual entry!
